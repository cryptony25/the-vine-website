Training Services

Every service starts with a conversation about where you are and where you want to go. Pricing and availability are shared during your free consultation.

One-on-One Personal Training

Private sessions built entirely around your goals, your schedule, and your starting point. Best for clients who want hands-on coaching and close attention to form.

  • Individual assessment and goal setting
  • Sessions tailored to your experience level
  • Ongoing adjustments as you progress

Small Group Training

Train alongside a few others in a supportive setting. A good fit for friends, couples, or anyone who stays motivated by community.

  • Small group sizes for real coaching attention
  • Shared encouragement and accountability
  • A more affordable way to train consistently

Custom Programming

A written training plan you follow on your own time, with check-ins to keep you on track. Ideal if you train at your own gym or travel often.

  • A program designed around your equipment and schedule
  • Regular check-ins and plan updates
  • Guidance on progression and recovery
